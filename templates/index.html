Algorithm Integration

Mathematical Optimization & Cyber-Physical Design

SmartPark integrates rigorous graph theory, capacity-constrained network flows, and heuristic path planning into a production reservation backend.

Directed Road Network $G=(V, E)$

Extracted directly from OpenStreetMap covering a 1,400m radius in Gandhipuram, Coimbatore. Directed edges preserve one-way traffic restrictions and exact metric arc lengths.

Minimum-Cost Maximum-Flow (MCMF)

Formulated as a bipartite min-cost flow problem $\min \sum c_{ij} x_{ij}$ subject to capacity constraints $A_j$. Optimizes multi-vehicle assignments while guaranteeing non-overlapping slot allocation.

A* Search with Haversine Heuristic

Combines path cost $g(n)$ with an admissible and consistent spherical great-circle heuristic $h(n)$, reducing search space exploration by over 40% compared to standard Dijkstra search.
