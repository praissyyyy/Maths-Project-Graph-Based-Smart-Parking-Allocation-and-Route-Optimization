SmartPark – Gandhipuram

Graph-Based Smart Car Parking Allocation and Route Optimization with Online Slot Booking. Designed for the urban commercial corridor of Gandhipuram, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

M.Tech Cyber Security Academic Project
Mathematical Models
  • Directed Road Graph $G = (V, E)$
  • Dijkstra's Shortest Path
  • A* Search (Haversine Heuristic)
  • Minimum-Cost Maximum-Flow (MCMF)
  • First-Come, First-Served (FCFS)
Academic Transparency

Simulated Environment: 5 parking facilities (P1–P5) and 150 slots are simulated for mathematical optimization and academic evaluation. Road network is extracted directly from OpenStreetMap.

Course: Mathematics for Cyber Security
